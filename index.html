1. 2026 Evaluation Codes, MDM Matrix & G2211 Complexity

E/M Codes (99202–99215)

Leveled exclusively on Medical Decision Making (MDM) or total time. Time thresholds: 99204 (45-59 min), 99205 (60-74 min), 99213 (20-29 min), 99214 (30-39 min), 99215 (40-54 min).

  • 99213 / 99203: Low MDM. 1 stable chronic illness (e.g. well-controlled dry eye) or 1 acute uncomplicated illness.
  • 99214 / 99204: Moderate MDM. 1 chronic illness with progression/side effects OR prescription drug management (initiating, modifying, or refilling glaucoma drops, NSAIDs, steroids).
  • 99215 / 99205: High MDM. Acute threat to vision (e.g., macula-on retinal detachment, acute angle closure, ischemic stroke amaurosis).
HCPCS G2211 Complexity Add-On

CMS reimburses ~$16.40 nationally on top of qualifying office E/M visits (99202–99215) for managing longitudinal, complex care.

  • Eligible: When the optometrist is the ongoing focal point for chronic disease (POAG, diabetic eye tracking, severe keratoconjunctivitis).
  • The Cardinal Prohibition: G2211 CANNOT be reported when the E/M code has modifier -25 appended for a same-day minor procedure.
  • Eye Codes (92002–92014): Governed by exam elements (external, biomicroscopy, fundus, motility, fields), not MDM. Commercial medical payers often reimburse 92014 higher than 99214.

2. Clinical Ophthalmic Modifiers: Clinical Vignettes

-25 (Significant, Separate E/M)

Vignette: Patient presents for scheduled glaucoma follow-up. During the slit lamp exam, a foreign body with rust ring is noted in the right cornea. The doctor performs an exam and removes the FB.

Billing: 99214-25, 65222-RT.

-59 / -XU (Distinct Procedural Service)

Vignette: Patient with dry AMD has an OCT macula performed. During dilation, a suspicious pigmented choroidal nevus is found in the far periphery, prompting fundus photography.

Billing: 92134 (Pointer to H35.31), 92250-59 (Pointer to H31.10).

-55 (Post-Operative Co-Management)

Vignette: Cataract surgeon performs uncomplicated phaco with IOL (66984). Care is transferred to the OD on post-op day 1 for the remainder of the 90-day global period.

Billing: 66984-55-RT (Box 19 notes: Transfer date 10/02/2026 to 12/31/2026).

-E1 to -E4 (Lid Modifiers on 68761)

Vignette: Patient with dry eye fails artificial tears and topical cyclosporine. Silicone punctal plugs are inserted into both lower puncta.

Billing: Line 1: 68761-E2 (Lower Right), Line 2: 68761-E4-51 (Lower Left).

3. Carrier-Specific Audit Commandments: Top 10 Medical & Vision Plans

Top 10 Medical Carriers

1. Medicare Part B (CMS): Enforces LCD L33751/L34648. OCT/VF capped at 1x per 6 months for stable disease. Requires -GY on refraction (92015). G2211 supported.
2. Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS): Reimburses ~115%-130% of Medicare. Frequently favors comprehensive eye codes (92014) over 99214. Bilateral laser requires modifier -50.
3. UnitedHealthcare (UHC): Strict commercial medical policies. Mandatory prior authorization for amniotic membranes (65778) and corneal cross-linking (0402T).
4. Aetna: Requires documented failure of conservative therapy (minimum 3 months) before punctal plug approval. Reimburses telehealth with modifier -95.
5. Cigna: Strict NCCI adherence. Rejects OCT (92134) and fundus photos (92250) billed on same day without distinct clinical indication.
6. Humana (Commercial & Medicare Adv): High Medicare Advantage presence. Enforces CMS Local Coverage Determinations strictly.
7. State Medicaid: Strict routine exam caps (typically once every 24 months for adults). Pediatric exams covered annually.
8. TRICARE: Covers medical eye exams and disease treatment. Routine eye exams covered for active duty family members once yearly.
9. VA Community Care (VA CCN): Requires approved standard episode of care (SEOC) authorization number in Box 23 of CMS-1500.
10. Medicare Advantage (Part C): Replaces traditional Medicare rules with commercial network edits. May require referrals from primary care.

Top 10 Managed Vision Plans

1. VSP Vision Care: Routine exams billed under 92004/92014 with Z01.00. Medical care allowed under Primary Eyecare Plan (PEP) rider for minor anterior segment conditions.
2. EyeMed Vision Care: Routine vision only. Medical diagnostic testing (OCT, VF, Photos) is strictly non-covered and must be carved out to major medical.
3. Davis Vision: Standard fee schedules for exam + refraction. Materials billed via Davis lab network. Medically necessary contact lenses require prior auth with K-readings.
4. Superior Vision: Covers comprehensive eye exam and refraction. Strict frequency limits (typically 12 months for exam, 24 months for frames).
5. Spectera (UHC Vision): Administers routine vision for UHC commercial plans. Cannot bill medical testing to Spectera; must route to UHC Medical.
6. Anthem Blue View Vision: Administered as a routine vision rider. Bundles refraction into primary exam allowance depending on employer group contract.
7. Aetna Vision Preferred: Powered by EyeMed network. Routine eye exams and materials only. All surgical and glaucoma procedures carved out.
8. Humana Vision: Frequently bundled with Medicare Advantage wellness benefits. Covers standard annual routine exam and hardware discount.
9. Vision Benefits of America (VBA): Commercial non-profit vision plan. Provides fixed reimbursement allowances for exam and materials.
10. March Vision Care: High-volume Medicaid and D-SNP vision plan. Prior authorization mandatory for specialty lenses and replacement hardware.
