1. E/M vs. Eye Codes

E/M Codes (99202–99215) are determined by Medical Decision Making (MDM) OR Total Time. MDM relies on the number/complexity of problems addressed, data reviewed, and risk.

  • 99213: Low risk. 1 stable chronic illness.
  • 99214: Moderate risk. 1 progressing illness OR Rx drug management (e.g. prescribing/refilling Latanoprost).
  • 99215: High risk. Severe exacerbation, threat to vision (e.g. acute macula-off RD).

Eye Codes (92002-92014) are determined by the exam elements performed (e.g. dilation, gross visual fields) regardless of MDM. Often favored by commercial plans.

2. Audit-Defense Rules

  • The CC Rule: Never bill a medical exam if the Chief Complaint is "needs new glasses." Document a medical symptom or transition of care.
  • I&R Mandate: Every single diagnostic test (OCT, VF, Photo, B-Scan) MUST have a distinct Interpretation & Report in the chart. "Test normal" is an automatic audit failure.
  • Bilateral Trap: Codes 92250 (Photo), 76514 (Pach), and 92060 (Sensorimotor) are inherently bilateral. Never append -RT/-LT or bill 2 units.
  • Frequency Caps: Most MACs limit stable glaucoma OCT/VFs to once every 6 months. Billing < 6 months requires documented progression.

3. Essential Ophthalmic Modifiers

-25 Separate E/M

Append to E/M when doing a distinct exam on the same day as a minor procedure (e.g., Exam + Punctal Plugs).

-59 / -XU Distinct Service

Bypasses NCCI edits when billing mutually exclusive tests on the same day for different conditions (e.g. OCT Macula + Fundus Photo for Nevus).

-55 Post-Op Only

Used for co-management (e.g. post-cataract care). You retain ~20% of the global fee based on transfer date.

-E1 to -E4 Lid Modifiers

Mandatory for punctal occlusion (68761) and epilation. E1=UR, E2=LR, E3=UL, E4=LL.

-GY Statutorily Excluded

Append to Refraction (92015) when submitting to Medicare to generate an official denial for secondary crossover.

-95 Telehealth

Append to E/M codes when encounter is performed via synchronous audio/video telecommunications (POS 02).
